Selected for the Kavli Science and Society Sparks Program

I was selected for the Kavli Foundation's Science and Society Sparks Program in public engagement, which runs from November to December 2026.

Nervous systems are wonderful material for public engagement. During my Ph.D. I developed and taught K-12 and adult-learner outreach curricula in biodiversity, genetics, and marine science across Florida, in a University of Florida collaboration with SeaKeepers, and I have spoken on cephalopod brain evolution at the Monterey Bay Aquarium and at TONMOCON, the cephalopod enthusiasts' conference.

A unit on how nervous systems handle environmental change, developed with local school students taking part, is the outreach project that I plan to build next.

The rest of my public science and outreach is on the Horizons page.
